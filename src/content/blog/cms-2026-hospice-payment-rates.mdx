The Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) has released the final rule for hospice payment rates in FY 2026. This update brings significant changes that every hospice agency needs to understand.

## Key Changes

The FY 2026 hospice payment update includes a net increase of approximately 2.9% to the hospice payment rates. This adjustment reflects the hospital market basket update, reduced by a productivity adjustment.

## What This Means For Your Agency

For the average hospice agency, this translates to increased reimbursement per patient day across all levels of care. Agencies that have been struggling with thin margins may find some relief, though costs continue to rise.

## Action Steps

1. Review your current per-diem costs against the new rates
2. Update your financial projections for the fiscal year
3. Assess staffing models to ensure alignment with expected volume
4. Review your billing processes to capture all eligible charges
