Claim denials are an inevitable part of medical billing, but they do not have to mean lost revenue. With a structured approach, you can recover the majority of denied claims.

## The Cost of Denials

Industry data shows that the average denial rate is 5-10% of all claims submitted. For a mid-size practice, that can represent hundreds of thousands of dollars in potential lost revenue annually.

## Our 5-Step Denial Management Process

### Step 1: Immediate Identification
Denials should be identified and categorized within 24 hours of receipt. Categorize by denial reason code to identify patterns.

### Step 2: Root Cause Analysis
Determine why the claim was denied. Common reasons include missing information, authorization issues, coding errors, and timely filing.

### Step 3: Corrective Action
Address the specific denial reason. This may involve correcting codes, obtaining missing documentation, or filing an appeal.

### Step 4: Timely Resubmission
Resubmit corrected claims or file appeals within payer-specific timeframes. Track all resubmissions.

### Step 5: Prevention
Use denial data to identify and fix systemic issues. The best denial management is denial prevention.
