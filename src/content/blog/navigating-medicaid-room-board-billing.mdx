Billing Medicaid for room and board for hospice patients in nursing facilities is one of the most complex and often overlooked areas of hospice billing.

## Understanding Room and Board

When a hospice patient resides in a nursing facility, the hospice is responsible for the patient overall plan of care. However, Medicaid should cover room and board costs separately from the Medicare hospice benefit.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Verify Medicaid eligibility** for the patient
2. **Determine the appropriate rate** based on state Medicaid guidelines
3. **Submit claims to Medicaid** with proper coding
4. **Coordinate with the nursing facility** for billing alignment
5. **Follow up on denials** promptly

## Common Pitfalls

- Not billing Medicaid at all (many agencies miss this entirely)
- Using incorrect rate codes for the state
- Failing to follow up on denied claims
- Not coordinating with the nursing facility billing department
