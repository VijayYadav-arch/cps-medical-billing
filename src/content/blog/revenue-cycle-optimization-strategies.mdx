Revenue cycle management (RCM) is the end-to-end process of managing patient care episodes from registration through final payment. Top-performing practices achieve 98%+ collection rates and under 30 days in A/R. Here are the 12 strategies they employ.

## Front-End Strategies (Before Service)

### 1. Real-Time Eligibility Verification
Verify insurance coverage, co-pays, deductibles, and prior authorization requirements before every service. Modern systems integrate directly with payers for instant verification.

**Impact**: Reduces downstream denials by 30-40%

### 2. Accurate Registration Processes
Train front-desk staff to capture complete and accurate patient information at registration. Small errors (transposed digits in member IDs, outdated addresses) create downstream billing problems.

**Impact**: Improves clean claim rate by 10-15%

### 3. Prior Authorization Management
Implement a proactive prior authorization process for services that require them. Many denials result from missing or expired authorizations.

**Impact**: Eliminates 100% of authorization-related denials when properly executed

## Mid-Cycle Strategies (Clinical Documentation)

### 4. Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI)
Work with physicians to ensure clinical documentation supports the most accurate and specific codes. This is particularly important for diagnosis-based payment models like PDGM and hospice.

**Impact**: Increases revenue 5-15% through improved coding specificity

### 5. Template-Based Documentation with Flexibility
Balance the efficiency of templates with the specificity needed for billing. Templates should prompt for all elements required for coding while allowing narrative detail.

**Impact**: Reduces documentation time while maintaining billing accuracy

### 6. Charge Capture Reconciliation
Ensure every service provided is captured and billed. Services that go unbilled represent pure lost revenue. Daily reconciliation between clinical visits and billed charges identifies gaps.

**Impact**: Recovers 2-5% of missed revenue annually

## Back-End Strategies (Claims Processing)

### 7. Claim Scrubbing Before Submission
Use claim scrubbing software to identify errors before submission. Every error caught pre-submission saves 15-30 days in the resolution cycle.

**Impact**: Improves first-pass claim acceptance to 95%+

### 8. Timely Claim Submission
Submit clean claims within 48-72 hours of service completion. Every day of delay is a day of lost revenue.

**Impact**: Reduces days in A/R by 10-15 days

### 9. Payment Posting and Reconciliation
Post payments accurately and promptly. Reconcile electronic remittance advice (ERA) with expected payments to identify underpayments.

**Impact**: Identifies 3-7% in underpayments for recovery

## Denial Management Strategies

### 10. Systematic Denial Categorization
Track denials by category: medical necessity, bundling, authorization, timely filing, coding, other. Pattern recognition reveals systemic issues.

**Impact**: Enables prevention of recurring denials

### 11. 72-Hour Denial Response
Respond to denials within 72 hours. Most denials have strict appeal deadlines, and faster response improves success rates.

**Impact**: Increases denial recovery rate to 65%+

### 12. Root Cause Analysis
For every denial, identify the root cause: coding error, documentation gap, process failure, or payer issue. Address root causes, not symptoms.

**Impact**: Reduces denial rates over time from 10% to under 5%

Track these KPIs monthly:
