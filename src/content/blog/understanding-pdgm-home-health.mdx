The Patient-Driven Groupings Model (PDGM) fundamentally changed how home health agencies are reimbursed by Medicare. Understanding its nuances is critical to maintaining healthy revenue.

## How PDGM Works

PDGM classifies patients into payment groups based on clinical characteristics, functional impairment levels, and referral source. Each 30-day period of care is paid at a specific rate.

## Maximizing Reimbursement

### Accurate Coding
The diagnosis codes used directly affect payment grouping. Ensure your clinicians document thoroughly and coders capture the full clinical picture.

### Timely OASIS Submissions
Late or inaccurate OASIS assessments can result in incorrect payment grouping. Establish quality assurance processes.

### LUPA Prevention
Low Utilization Payment Adjustments can significantly reduce payment. Monitor visit patterns to ensure you meet the minimum threshold.
